# Product brief

## Objective
Turn a visitor interested in eyelid and eye-area concerns into an informed consultation enquiry. Establish the doctor’s specialist identity and make clinic selection clear. Primary journey: understand specialty → explore approved information → choose clinic → follow verified booking destination.

## Audience hypothesis
Arabic-speaking visitors in Riyadh interested in eye and eyelid appearance and care. Female imagery appeared in the reviewed Instagram subset, but the site must not restrict care to women or assert audience demographics without analytics. Use gender-neutral Arabic wherever practical.

## Scope
V1: home, about, services index, approved service details, journal index and approved articles, FAQ, booking/locations, privacy, 404. Arabic only. No user accounts, payments, consultation chat, diagnosis quiz, patient upload or appointment database.

## Brand promise as editorial direction
«تفاصيل صغيرة. اهتمام دقيق.» This is a proposed headline, not a medical outcome claim. Tone: calm, precise, warm, confident without exaggeration. Avoid: الأفضل، نتائج مضمونة، بلا مخاطر، أحدث تقنية عالميًا، سنوات من الخبرة without substantiation.

## Success
Visitors understand the specialty within the first viewport; can reach booking within two intentional actions; mobile pages feel composed rather than compressed. Measure actual external booking clicks after integration and privacy decisions; do not infer completed appointments from clicks.
